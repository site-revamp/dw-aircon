# DW Aircon Servicing Singapore Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

DW Aircon Servicing Singapore provides aircon servicing, maintenance, repairs, installation, and specialised cleaning for homes and businesses across Singapore. The proposed homepage would help visitors find the right service, review prices and past work, and contact the team.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make contact details consistent.** The existing site lists different phone numbers and operating hours across pages. The redesign would use confirmed phone numbers and clearly labelled hotline and support hours. It would identify WhatsApp as a messaging channel and make clear that the main office does not accept walk-ins.
2. **Simplify service navigation.** The existing menu mixes services, product catalogues, guides, and promotions across several levels. The proposed homepage would give servicing, repairs, installation, and commercial aircon work clear entry points, with direct links to relevant service details and enquiries.
3. **Give pricing and evidence of past work more visibility.** The existing site has published servicing prices, customer reviews, and project examples that are easy to miss. The proposed layout would put servicing rates in a scannable section and give verified Google reviews and completed work dedicated space so visitors can assess cost and service quality before enquiring.

## Design Improvements

Clear section headings and concise service summaries would make the homepage easier to scan. Prominent enquiry buttons beside service and pricing information would give visitors a clear next step.
